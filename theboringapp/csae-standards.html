Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation

Last updated: 14 Sep 2026

The Boring App has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind, in any part of the app or its associated services.

1. Our Commitment

The Boring App, as published on Google Play, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This applies to every surface of the app — including usernames, leaderboard profiles, and any user-submitted content — and to our support channels.

2. How We Define CSAE

We adopt Google Play's definition: CSAE is any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children. This includes, without limitation:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Sexualization of minors, including in text, images, or usernames
  • Grooming — befriending or contacting a minor to lower their inhibitions for future sexual contact
  • Sextortion — threatening or coercing a minor into providing sexual content or engaging in sexual activity
  • Sex trafficking of children
  • Any other conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child

3. Enforcement

Because The Boring App collects minimal user data — sessions can be run anonymously and no messaging or media-sharing exists in the app — the surface area for this kind of abuse is small. Where user-facing text does exist (such as leaderboard usernames), we remove violating content on discovery and permanently ban the associated account.
